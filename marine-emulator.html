01 · THE CHALLENGE

Complex ecosystem models are scientifically powerful—but computationally demanding.

Atlantis represents multispecies interactions, fishing pressure, environmental variability, and spatial ecosystem structure. That ecological realism is valuable for ecosystem analysis, but repeated simulations can require substantial computation and limit rapid scenario exploration.

ATLANTIS
RESEARCH QUESTION Can machine learning learn Atlantis ecosystem behavior and deliver accurate biomass predictions and forecasts at a fraction of the computational cost?

07 · WHY IT MATTERS

Surrogate modeling turns expensive simulation into rapid scientific experimentation.

The emulator is designed as a computationally efficient approximation of Atlantis, not as a replacement for mechanistic ecosystem science.
